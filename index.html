Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about the study club and its educational programs.

Who are the courses designed for?

The courses are designed primarily for dentists and dental professionals who want to develop, strengthen, or refresh knowledge and clinical skills in dental sedation and related areas of practice.

What types of courses are available?

Programs include nitrous oxide–oxygen minimal sedation certification, oral moderate sedation certification, sedation refresher courses, IV sedation refresher training, and continuing education on medical emergencies.

How are courses delivered?

Programs with a clinical component may use a hybrid format, combining online didactic learning with in-person clinical training. Some courses are delivered online.
